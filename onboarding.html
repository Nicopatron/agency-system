Diana’s agency
system.

Boutique real estate operations in eight specialists — six pipeline stages plus two off-pipeline aggregators. Built so the newest agent on your team can pick it up in a day. Refuse-first by design.

It’s Tuesday, 10:45 p.m. A client texts asking where the option period stands. You’re mid-dinner; you dig through three Google Docs and two email threads to answer a yes/no question. This system changes that. Each folder is one specialist. Together they cover lead intake through live deal tracking — handing off through structured contracts, not handwaved prose.

Architecture

Four ideas that make it
a system, not a folder of prompts.

01.

One start point.

The orchestrator routes ambiguous requests to the right specialist. Senior agents who know where they’re going skip it and go direct.

02.

Context travels.

Every handoff is a typed YAML packet. The output of one specialist is the exact input shape the next one accepts — no translation layer.

03.

Nothing reaches the agent unreviewed.

Every draft passes through quality review before the agent sees it — checked against four criteria. Approve, revise, or escalate.

04.

Voice stays yours.

Each agent sets up a voice profile once at onboarding (~20 min). The communication specialist reads it at draft time. Diana sounds like Diana.

The Eight

What each specialist owns.

One folder per specialist. Each carries an identity.md, rules.md, examples.md, and a typed handoff.md. The first six (00–05) form the inbound pipeline; the last two (06 and 07) are off-pipeline aggregators triggered manually by an agent. Click into any specialist to see operational discipline.

00

Orchestrator

Front door — routes ambiguous requests to the right specialist.

Accepts
Raw paste — any forwarded client email, hallway note, or “what do I do with this”.
Produces
routed_request YAML with intent, target specialist, prepared input, and a confidence anchor that caps everything downstream.
Use when
Junior agent on any request, or compound request (new lead arrives with a property question pinned to it).
Skip when
Senior agent with a clear request type — go direct to the target specialist.
Operational discipline
Owns
Five intent classes · routing matrix · multi-stage chain sequencing.
Refusal
Out-of-scope requests (legal, broker arbitration, non-Austin) refuse cleanly and escalate to Diana plus the right external channel.
01

Lead Qualifier

Turns raw prospect signals into a structured qualified_lead.

Accepts
Raw lead signals — web form, referral, cold call, open house signup, past-client tap.
Produces
qualified_lead YAML with five-input intake score and confidence cap that propagates downstream.
Use when
Any new prospect arrives, regardless of source.
Refuses when
Fewer than three of five intake inputs captured. Returns gap list and recovery questions.
Operational discipline
Gate
Five inputs: intent · budget · timeline · location · constraints.
Confidence
5/5 caps at 95 · 4/5 caps at 80 · 3/5 caps at 65 · ≤2/5 refuses outright.
02

Property Research

Structured research_brief on Austin properties, neighborhoods, and market data.

Accepts
qualified_lead from 01, or a routed_request from 00 with intent property_question.
Produces
research_brief — comparables, findings (each with evidence and confidence), recommendation for communication.
Use when
Need comps, a neighborhood scan, a specific-property CMA, or market-segment context.
Coverage
Austin metro only — Travis, Hays, Williamson, Bastrop. Anything outside refuses with a referral.
Operational discipline
Owns
Source citation on every claim · Fair Housing rule on schools (links to TEA only) · domain-fact-pending.md catch file.
Penalties
−15 if comparables count below 3 · −10 if school ratings unverifiable · −10 if data older than 60 days.
03

Client Communication

Drafts in the agent’s voice — reads the cached voice_profile.md, never auto-sends.

Accepts
Output from any other specialist — qualified_lead, research_brief, deal_event, or a direct routed_request.
Produces
comm_draft with send checklist. For acceptance comms, also a deal_seed initializing 04.
Use when
Anything client-facing — first touch, inspection issue, competing offer, financing update.
Refuses when
No voice_profile.md available and no signing_agent_fallback invoked.
Operational discipline
Routes to
05_quality_review automatically before the agent sees the draft. No exceptions.
Forbidden
leverage, unlock, navigate, streamline, empower, seamless, synergy, circle back, reach out, best practices.
04

Transaction Coordinator

Tracks active deals from contract to close — surfaces risks 48h before deadlines.

Accepts
deal_seed from 03, or deal_update blocks (status checks, new events).
Produces
deal_state — parties, key dates, doc checklist, risk register, history. Emits deal_event when comms are needed.
Use when
Contract is executed. Does not touch pre-acceptance work — that belongs to 01, 02, and 03.
Operational discipline
Owns
TREC 20-18 milestone reference · current_day_in_contract · append-only history log.
Risk flags
Option period expiring without inspection booked · financing contingency approaching with lender silent · appraisal-gap risk.
05

Quality Review

Last specialist before a draft reaches the agent. Returns notes; never rewrites.

Accepts
comm_draft with drafted_by: "03_client_communication" — no other input shape accepted.
Produces
quality_verdict — verdict (approved / revise / escalate) plus per-criterion notes.
Four criteria
Specificity · Clarity · Brevity (≤200 words) · Voice (matches profile).
Loop-back
Revise routes back to 03 with notes. Maximum two cycles. Then escalates to Diana.
Operational discipline
Escalation log
Every escalation logged in escalation-log.md. Diana reviews periodically → promotes patterns to team-standards.md.
06

Daily Brief

Morning sync — read-only aggregator across every active workflow.

Accepts
Manual trigger from any team agent (“Run morning brief”). Off-pipeline — never invoked via routed_request.
Produces
morning_brief in structured markdown — URGENT (today) · Diana’s decision queue · IN PROGRESS · stale alerts (>72h) · pipeline snapshot.
Use when
Start of every workday, before client touches. The team reads it together — one rollup replaces four separate CRM scrolls.
Writes
Nothing. Pure read aggregation across workflows/*/status.md, action_register.md, audit_log.md, and escalation-log.md.
Operational discipline
Owns
72h staleness threshold · slip-color aggregation (🔴 RED / 🟡 YELLOW / 🔵 BLUE / 🟢 GREEN) · pipeline snapshot math · deterministic five-section structure.
Never
Writes to any file (read-only by design) · invents action recommendations (every “Next:” line is pulled from existing workflow state) · runs unattended on a schedule (the daily ritual IS the synchronization).
07

Nurture Coordinator

Long-horizon cadence for not-ready-yet leads & past clients.

Accepts
Manual trigger from agent (“Run nurture coordinator”), or a qualified_lead from 01 with nurture_intake: true (timeline >6 months out).
Produces
nurture_touch_plan · graduation_candidate (back to 01 for re-qualification) · drop_request (manual confirmation required).
Use when
Weekly nurture sweep, or specific past-client check-in (e.g., 6-week post-close touch, anniversary, life event triggered).
Routes to
03_client_communication drafts every touch — then through 05_quality_review like any other outbound. No bypass.
Operational discipline
Owns
Cadence tiers (monthly / quarterly / annual) · graduation triggers (timeline <90 days, life event, explicit re-engagement, matching listing) · drop criteria (4 consecutive misses, unsubscribe, out-of-area, adversarial termination).
Never
Drafts comms (that’s 03) · auto-sends · asks for referrals in early touches · proposes generic “just checking in” without specific reference points from the workflow.

One lead, four stages.

Tom and Priya Patel are relocating from San Francisco to Austin. Their inquiry travels through the system end to end. Pick a stage:

Confidence ●●●●●●●●○○ 80 / 100

Why 80, not 95. Four of five intake inputs are captured — must-haves and deal-breakers are still missing. Every downstream specialist will cap its own confidence at eighty until that fifth input is collected. The system flags it; it doesn’t silently assume.

Why it dropped from 80 to 65. The brief is capped at the upstream qualified lead’s eighty, then reduced by fifteen because comparables count is below the three-comp threshold. The system degrades confidence honestly downstream, instead of masking the gap.

Proactive, not reactive. The SF bank pre-qualification was the only financing signal in the file. Day 1, the system flagged it. If lender clarity goes unresolved to day 20 of a 21-day financing contingency, the deal is at risk. The flag is created before it becomes a missed deadline — that is the entire point of this folder existing.

Getting Started

Five paths.
All produce the same contracts.

Pick the one that matches your workflow. The output discipline is identical — only the surface changes.

AClaude Projects ~5 min·best role separation +

Eight Claude Projects, one per specialist. Clearest role separation — recommended for teams who want each agent to work in their own focused workspace.

  1. Clone or download this folder.
  2. Open claude.ai → New Project. Create eight projects named 00-orchestrator, 01-lead-qualifier, 02-property-research, 03-client-communication, 04-transaction-coordinator, 05-quality-review, 06-daily-brief, 07-nurture-coordinator.
  3. For each project, upload identity.md, rules.md, examples.md, handoff.md into Project Knowledge. Always include _config/team-standards.md.
  4. Extras: 02 and 04 add domain-fact-pending.md; 05 adds ../escalation-log.md.
  5. For 03, each agent sets up their own voice-profiles/<name>.md from the template. About twenty minutes per agent; refresh every ninety days.
  6. Open a chat in any workspace, paste your situation, and ask: “Act as 01_lead_qualifier and produce a qualified_lead.”
BClaude Code ~2 min·full file access +

Run the system locally via Claude Code. Best for technical agents who want to inspect files, run variations, and iterate fast.

  1. Clone the repository:
    $ git clone https://github.com/Nicopatron/agency-system.git $ cd agency-system
  2. Open the folder in Claude Code. It auto-reads CLAUDE.md → AGENTS.md.
  3. Tell it the situation and the specialist:
    › "Read this folder. New lead: [paste Patel text]. Act as 01_lead_qualifier and produce the qualified_lead."
  4. Continue the chain: “Pass this to 02_property_research and produce a research_brief.”
CCodex / Cursor / Windsurf ~2 min·agent-agnostic +

Any agent that auto-discovers AGENTS.md — Codex CLI, Cursor, Windsurf, Zed, Roo Code.

  1. Clone the repository:
    $ git clone https://github.com/Nicopatron/agency-system.git $ cd agency-system
  2. Open in your agent. Most read AGENTS.md on session start.
  3. Paste your situation. Same output contract as paths A and B.

Onboarding

From zero to solo on 01,
in one day.

The bar is “newest agent operational in one day”. Here is what that day looks like.

Morning ~ 1 hr
Read this manual top to bottom. Skim each folder’s identity.md — eight files, five minutes each.
Understand what each specialist owns. Know when to go direct versus route through the orchestrator.
Mid-day ~ 2 hrs
Walk through onboarding/patel-scenario.md end to end. Run each stage yourself; compare your output to the expected output shown.
See one complete lead flow through all six pipeline specialists (00→05). Understand confidence propagation and the quality gate in action. Run a morning brief via 06_daily_brief on Day 2 to see the read-only aggregator pattern.
Afternoon ~ 2 hrs
Pair with a senior agent on a real live lead. Senior copies handoffs between projects; junior reads outputs and asks where divergence happens.
See the system on real data. Build intuition for what “good” looks like before going solo.
End of day ~ 30 min
Junior takes one stage solo — start with 01_lead_qualifier. Paste a real or test lead; senior reviews the qualified_lead output.
First solo output. If it produces a clean qualified lead with the right confidence, the agent is ready for Day 2.
Day 2 onwards ongoing
Junior runs 01 → 02 → 03 on real leads with senior approval at each handoff. By Day 5, solo on routine intake.
Full pipeline fluency. The agent now understands the contract discipline well enough to catch their own mistakes before 05 does.
Set up your voice profile During Day 1, each agent should also set up their voice-profiles/<name>.md. Open voice-profiles/_template.md, follow the prompts (sentence style, openings, closings, phrases to avoid), and pull in three to five past emails as reference. About twenty minutes. From that point forward, the communication specialist drafts in your voice — not the team’s generic house style.
