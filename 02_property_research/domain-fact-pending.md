## Verified facts (graduated 2026-05-12)

### School rating data sources (verified HIGH)
**Standard practice for Austin boutique RE teams:** reference BOTH sources, do NOT characterize.
- **TEA (Texas Education Agency)** — [txschools.gov](https://txschools.gov/) — official A-F accountability ratings for districts and campuses (Austin ISD, Eanes ISD, Round Rock ISD, Leander ISD). Methodology: [tea.texas.gov/.../a-f-accountability](https://tea.texas.gov/texas-schools/accountability/academic-accountability/a-f-accountability).
- **GreatSchools.org** — consumer-facing 1-10 ratings that appear in Zillow / Redfin / Realtor.com listings (the rating the buyer already saw).

**🔴 Fair Housing rule (non-negotiable):** TREC and NAR guidance prohibit the agent from characterizing schools as "good" or "bad". Boutique-team practice in 2026: provide LINKS to both sources and let the client interpret. Do not opine.

### 78704 median price-per-sqft (verified MEDIUM)
- **Median 78704 (overall, March 2026, Redfin):** $468/sqft, **down 19.3% YoY**.
- **Median 78704 sale price (March 2026):** $798K.
- **Implication:** the $650-750K bracket is BELOW the 78704 median. Market is in contraction (4 consecutive years of decline through April 2026).

### Inspection sequence (verified HIGH)
Inspections (general + specialty: foundation, roof, HVAC, termite, environmental) happen during the option period. This is universal TX practice — the option period exists for this purpose.
