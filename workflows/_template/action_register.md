## Rules

- Every state-changing request that produces a required follow-up gets a row here.
- One row per action — do not bundle. If two things need to happen, two rows.
- Owner is specific: "Diana" or "Marcus" or "Capitol Federal" — not "agent".
- Completed actions stay in the register. Delete nothing.
- Read-only questions ("what are my open tasks?") do not add rows.
